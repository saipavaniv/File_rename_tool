🐇 The Hop button (Chrome / Edge)

One click renames every file on the page you're looking at and downloads it. Setting it up takes a minute:

  1. Open chrome://extensions in a new tab and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder inside your Hop folder.
  3. A tab opens asking to connect. Click Allow. Done!

Then click 🐇 in the toolbar (pin it with the puzzle-piece icon) or press Alt+Shift+R on any page with files.
